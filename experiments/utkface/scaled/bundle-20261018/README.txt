UTKFace seven-figure bundle

Open viewer.html in a browser. It is self-contained and needs no network connection. Use the seven page links, Plotly legend clicks/double-clicks, zoom/pan tools, and the axis range controls. The full-data curves aggregate seeds 42/43/44. Other panels state their actual coverage. No new training or test-driven selection was performed.

Default moving means are five epochs, or ten for 200-epoch runs. Choose Raw to inspect unsmoothed curves. Bands are sample SD across optimization seeds, not confidence intervals. Initial axis limits focus on later fit; Fit all data includes early transients. Scalar label NLL is undefined.

Approved coverage adaptations: SD 1/3/5/10; eight available categorical 1024x200 settings; categorical MAE/MSE replace unavailable scalar/sampled-TailRL baselines in the focused 1024x200 row. Eta is noise RMS / signal SD; conventional SNR = 1/eta^2.

Each figure is supplied as PNG and vector PDF. all-seven.pdf contains all seven pages. bundle-data.json records source IDs, LR values, seeds, raw curves and validation-only selection decisions.
