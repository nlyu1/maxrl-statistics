# UTKFace final interpretation

The selected study is complete: **514 jobs**, including **222 scientific final evaluations**, using **65.265 GPU-hours**. Scientific snapshot: October 16, 2026, 14:47 GMT+8. [Detailed final report](FINAL.md), [interactive report](dashboard.html), [all tables and selected epochs](FINAL_TABLES.md).

Scalar MAE now has the lowest observed full-data three-seed MAE, **4.632 ± 0.023 years**, versus **4.718 ± 0.086** for population Stat-MAE width 2 and **4.724 ± 0.019** for sampled TailRL. Its paired gap against Stat-MAE width 2 is −0.085 ± 0.070 years, improving on all three seeds. Three optimization seeds on one fixed split support a promising result, not a general superiority claim. Scalar heads do not provide categorical label probabilities. [Full-data scalar curves](final-scalar.pdf).

Population TailRL remains a useful distributional compromise: **4.752 ± 0.037 years MAE** and **3.214 nats label NLL**, versus 6.698 nats for sampled TailRL. Categorical mean-regression and narrow statistical objectives can have good point predictions and substantially worse likelihoods. Population estimators improve MAE in five of ten objective pairs and worsen it in five. The complete 66-run categorical matrix is unchanged. [Main table](MAIN_MATRIX.md).

On 4096 images, three-seed categorical MSE and population TailRL reach **5.297 ± 0.174** and **5.359 ± 0.097**. Statistical MaxRL's favorable full-data ranking does not transfer to this setting. Categorical MAE at rate 0.003 is seed-sensitive: **5.576, 5.917, 8.074** years. The last run also has poor validation error, so it is retained rather than dismissed as a test anomaly. Rates chosen on a short single-seed pilot do not necessarily transfer across seeds. Small-subset scalar heads still have only one seed. [Replication plot](final-replication.pdf).

Extending 1024-image training to 200 epochs matches the 3200 updates and 204800 presentations of 4096 × 50, but does not close the error gap for any of the eight methods. Six worsen by more than 0.1 years relative to 1024 × 50 and two are practical ties. The longer horizon also changes warmup and cosine decay, and inherits a rate tuned on 20-epoch pilots. These are complete-setting comparisons, not a pure test of more updates. [Update-axis curves](final-small-subsets.pdf).

Constant LR has mixed selected-checkpoint test results: one better, four worse, five practical ties at the descriptive 0.1-year threshold. Final validation MAE worsens for nine of ten methods. Constant LR removes warmup as well as decay. Final-epoch test models were not retained. [Schedule endpoints](schedule.pdf).

All eighty noise settings are complete. Fresh noise improves on fixed noise for nine of ten methods at SD 5 and all ten at SD 10. At SD 5 it improves TailRL, CE and both statistical objectives relative to their own clean seed-42 baselines, while direct categorical MSE is nearly unchanged and scalar regression worsens. Fixed/fresh SNR is nearly equal at each requested SD. Temporal resampling therefore matters beyond noise magnitude, but one training seed and one noise field do not establish a mechanism or broad robustness. [Noise curves](noise.pdf), [SNR](snr.pdf).

All sixteen conditional digit-policy pilots are complete. Every 4096-image pilot improves observed MAE over its matched flat-head baseline, but full-data outcomes are mixed and NLL is not uniformly better. The largest subset MAE improvement also changes LR by tenfold. Head capacity and tuning grids differ; full-data digit runs reuse subset-tuned rates. These results do not isolate parameterization. [Both data sizes](final-digits.pdf).

Forty optional noise seed repetitions remain held and thirty-two independent-field experiments were excluded. All metrics use the same cleaned split, which removes exact RGB duplicates but does not establish person-level independence. Comparisons retain matching seeds, data/subset fingerprints, training horizons and training-source cohorts. Test models are selected on validation MAE; no test outcome was used to retune a learning rate.
