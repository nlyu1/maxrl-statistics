__QUESTIONS__

What the raggedness measures

Evaluation inputs and the validation set are fixed. Evaluation calls model.eval(), uses center crops, and computes exact policy mean/median/mode metrics. Learning-curve fluctuations describe different model checkpoints, with both weights and BatchNorm running statistics changing during training. The configured CUDA path allows nondeterministic algorithms; deterministic inputs do not guarantee identical training repeats.

The archived sharing probes repeat nominally identical four-epoch training configurations. Their spread measures short-run training repeatability under that probe protocol. It is not the standard error of the new 50-epoch study. Paired test-image bootstrap intervals condition on already trained models and their selected checkpoints; they do not measure training-seed or corruption-field uncertainty. The displayed 95% intervals are pointwise and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

A useful replication varies training randomness across paired method arms and independently varies corruption fields. Multiple head losses fitted on one backbone, neighboring epochs, and many test-image bootstrap draws do not create independent backbone replications.

__RANDOMNESS__

Earlier SL-versus-TailRL feature finding

The earlier audit directly compared SL-trained and TailRL-trained backbones on clean data and fixed SD10. The new 75-head sweep compares ImageNet, SL and CE backbones across five noise conditions. The earlier direct SL/Tail representation result and the new SL/CE source comparison answer related but distinct questions.

The earlier SD10 Tail-head advantage on SL features was 0.064 years. In the new sweep the MSE head wins on SL features at all five noise levels by 0.008–0.060 years. Protocol differences include newly trained source checkpoints, fixed versus augmented head inputs, and head initialization/order seeds (earlier 20261025, new 42/2026). These are different protocols, rather than repeated measurements of an identical fit.

For the earlier checkpoints, refitted readouts and ridge probes support a representation difference. Label-dependent shape gradients remain a proposed cause that requires an encoder-gradient intervention. Refitting changes head initialization, optimization budget and feature/input protocol together; its gain does not isolate freezing alone.
