How to read this comparison, checkpoint inventory, and reproducibility

What is compared. ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 with the same 100-age categorical head, 4,096 training images, 50 epochs, cosine schedule, optimization seed 42, subset seed 2026, and noise seed 2026. TailRL and both GRPO variants use their exact population objectives. GRPO-MAE is the default; sampled GRPO was not part of this noise study.

Rates. TailRL, CE, and GRPO-MAE use LR 0.001. GRPO-MSE uses LR 0.0003, as recorded in the existing study. The GRPO-MSE comparison therefore also differs in learning rate.

Checkpoint selection. Each saved best.pt minimizes clean validation MAE of the policy mean, among epochs 1–50. Epochs can differ across methods and noise settings. Policies are not selected for best likelihood, final-epoch behavior, or a common training epoch.

Labels. “Original” is the UTKFace dataset annotation. Gaussian training noise is added in years, rounded to the nearest integer with NumPy, and clipped to [0,99]. Fixed labels persist through all epochs; fresh labels are resampled once per epoch. No labels from held-out validation or test were corrupted during training.

Examples. The 32 training and 16 held-out test faces are sampled across age decades using seed 20261022, before evaluating any policy. Every setting uses the same faces. The balanced example batch is illustrative and does not represent the dataset's age frequencies. The training batch belongs to the actual 4,096-image training subset.

Inference. Deterministic evaluation crop: resize to 256, center-crop to 224, ImageNet normalization. The displayed crop is the input crop. The CNN executes in bfloat16, then float32 log-softmax produces probabilities. No temperature adjustment, smoothing, bin aggregation, or truncation is applied.

Plot scales. “Shared within each row” gives the three methods exactly the same y-axis range for that face and noise level; the range may differ between rows. “Absolute” fixes every chart to [0,1]. “Log” displays values down to 10⁻⁸; smaller values are visually floored, and the exact-bin inspector still reports the stored probability. Mean, median, mode, entropy H (nats), original-label probability, and original-label NLL describe each policy. Lines joining bins are not used.

Limits. One optimization seed and one corruption field per condition. These examples illustrate saved policies and do not establish statistical significance or general robustness. In the batch view, the summary above the plots uses all 2,308 clean test images from the original evaluation; per-face numbers use only the shown input. Test images have no corrupted training label. The epoch control displays a common epoch's labels, while each panel also records the label at that method's own checkpoint epoch for fresh noise.
